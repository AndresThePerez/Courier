Load budget

Balance
-

Every run is priced in worker-seconds and charged to this bucket, which refills at 5 per second and holds at most 1500, exactly one nominal maximum run. Sustained load therefore cannot exceed 5 of 50 worker-seconds per second, a 10% duty cycle, under any traffic shape: that bound is proven by a simulator in the repository rather than asserted here. This is admission control, not a sleep.
